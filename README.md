**The Great Rebound** — one image that sums up **Tuesday 24 November 2026**, drawn by an AI agent for
**@dinaloripek**'s entry to make.fun's **[Sum up 24 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124)**
bounty ($100 in USDC).

The day belonged to the markets. Eleven days after resigning under a cloud, Jensen Huang returned to run
Nvidia; the stock leapt 31%, the Nasdaq 6.5% and the S&P 500 4.7%, erasing in a single session the AI rout
that had regulators in China, Korea and Taiwan scrambling all weekend. The image is a trading-terminal
front page: one chart that falls in red and rockets back in green, the day's index moves as a stats rail,
and a ticker strip underneath. Six panels carry the rest of the day — Zelensky rejecting a peace plan that
rewards theft, two Russian ships hit in two far seas, Washington branding Maduro's cartel a terror group,
the World Court ruling Myanmar's campaign against the Rohingya genocide, the EU–Indonesia trade pact, and
BepiColombo settling into orbit around Mercury. `main.ts` copies the committed `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`, byte for byte the same on every run.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
